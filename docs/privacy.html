Privacy & participation

Your choices matter.

Read the information below before sharing personal details or joining a session.

Using the website

The guides and worksheet are available without an account. The site does not store journal responses or add advertising trackers. Your host may process technical requests.

External forms and messages

Booking links, WhatsApp, and Instagram open external services. Review their information before submitting details. Please avoid sending sensitive health information in an initial enquiry.

Video lessons

Where available, videos connect to YouTube only when you choose to play them.

Who is this space for?

HEAL is exclusively for girls and women. If you are under 18, please enquire with a parent or guardian about suitability and participation before booking.

Are sessions recorded?

The Exhale Room includes recordings. Ask about what is captured and how recordings are shared before joining. Participants should not record or take screenshots themselves.

Is coaching therapy?

Coaching supports reflection, decisions, and everyday habits. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
