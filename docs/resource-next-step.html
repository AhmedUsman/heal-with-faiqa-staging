Journaling / Free guide

One small step forward

Choose one action within your influence.

1. Name the situation

Write a short description of something occupying your mind. Stay close to what you know, rather than treating every worry as a fact.

2. Separate the parts

Make two columns: “Within my influence” and “Beyond my control.” Your preparation, words, and choices may be in the first column. Another person’s response or an uncertain outcome may be in the second.

3. Choose one action

Look at the first column. What is one step you can take with the time and resources you have? For example: ask one question, prepare one document, or set aside ten minutes to begin.

4. Make room for faith

If you wish, take a moment for dua in your own words. Reflect on approaching your responsibilities with care while placing your trust in Allah. You do not have to feel certain about the outcome before taking a thoughtful step.

5. Give the step a place

Write when you could begin and what would make it easier. “Tomorrow after breakfast, I will…” is more usable than a large promise to change everything.

For your notebook

What belongs to my effort today? What outcome am I trying to control that I cannot guarantee?
