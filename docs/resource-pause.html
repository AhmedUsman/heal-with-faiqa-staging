A five-minute pause

Notice what is asking for your attention today.

1. Arrive where you are

Set down what you can for a few minutes. Sit in a position that feels comfortable. You do not need to change your breathing or close your eyes; simply notice that you are here.

2. Name what is present

Try a simple sentence: “Right now, I notice…” You might notice tiredness, frustration, gratitude, uncertainty, or more than one feeling. Choose a word that fits rather than searching for the perfect answer.

3. Notice the demand

Ask yourself: “What is taking the most space in my mind today?” Name one thing. You do not need to list every responsibility or revisit a painful memory.

4. Consider what might help

Would a glass of water, a short rest, a clearer plan, a conversation, or a little quiet help? Think about what is possible in your present circumstances.

5. Choose something small

Complete this sentence: “For the next few minutes, I can…” Keep your answer modest. A pause can be worthwhile even when the wider situation has not changed.

For your notebook

What am I carrying today? What is one small act of care I can offer myself?
