Before you begin

Common questions

A few things you might want to know before joining.

Who is this space for?

HEAL is exclusively for girls and women. If you are under 18, please enquire with a parent or guardian about suitability and participation before booking.

Do I have to share my story?

No. Group exercises can be completed quietly in your own notebook. You decide what to discuss in individual coaching.

How do I book?

Choose an option on the booking page. When a form is available, follow its instructions for fees, availability, and confirmation. Please ask Faiqa if you need details first.

How do faith and NLP fit together?

Quranic learning grounds our reflections. Selected NLP-style coaching questions can help you examine a familiar assumption, try different wording, or choose a practical goal. They are not religious rulings or clinical treatments.

Is coaching therapy?

Coaching supports reflection, decisions, and everyday habits. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.

Are sessions recorded?

The Exhale Room includes recordings. Ask about what is captured and how recordings are shared before joining. Participants should not record or take screenshots themselves.
