A conversation with me

You can tell the long version.

Maybe you agreed to something you didn’t have time for. On the way home you kept thinking, Why did I say yes again?

I’d want to hear what happened in that moment. Were you worried about disappointing someone? Did you need more time to decide?

Then we might try out a reply together: “Let me check what I already have on this week.” You get to change the words until they sound like you.
