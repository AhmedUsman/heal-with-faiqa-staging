Meet your coach

Assalamu Alaikum,
I’m Faiqa Abdullah.

My starting point is the Qur’an. My work is about making room for its wisdom in the choices, feelings, and responsibilities of everyday life.

Why this practice exists

A place for honest questions.

My teaching background is in Islamic studies and Tajweed. Training in life coaching and NLP gave me another way to work with the questions women bring: How do I set a boundary? What can I do when a situation cannot change yet? How can I put my trust in Allah and still take action?

At HEAL, you can bring a question like that without needing to explain your whole life first.
